Trinsmo Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

The short version

Your journal is yours. It lives on your device. We never see it.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your evening pages (worries and strengths), your day’s list, and your settings are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple’s iCloud sync can keep them in your own iCloud account so your own iPhone, iPad, and Mac stay in step. That data is yours, in your account; the app’s makers cannot read it.
  • No account is needed. The app never asks you to sign up, and there is no server of ours holding anything.

What the app never does

  • No analytics, no trackers, no advertising identifiers.
  • No reading of your entries by anyone, ever, including us.
  • No selling, sharing, or transmitting of your data to anyone.

Photos and camera

Trinsmo never asks for your photos or your camera. There is nothing in the app that uses either one.

Notifications

The app sends none.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.
